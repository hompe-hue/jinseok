 Cold-Start Penalty 
An analysis was conducted on the EPA database of MY 2016 light-duty vehicles (U.S. EPA, 2016c) for the 
different fuel economy values reported for the different bags. Following an average of the ratio of Bag 3 
fuel economy over Bag 1 fuel economy, a 15% cold-start penalty was selected to be implemented. The 
cold-start penalty was applied for the fuel consumption during the FTP for conventional vehicles, HEVs, 
and PHEVs; 0% was applied for BEVs. Figure 181 shows the ratio of Bag 3 fuel economy to Bag 1 fuel 
economy. An average value of 15% was used as the cold-start penalty value for Bag 1. An additional 
penalty of 2.5% was applied to the Bag 2 fuel economy
